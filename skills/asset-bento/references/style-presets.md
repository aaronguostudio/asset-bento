# Style Presets

Built-in presets live in `presets/styles/`.

## Choosing Presets

Recommend only 2-3 presets at a time:

- Loading, empty state, success: `premium-minimal`, `friendly-clay`, `playful-mascot-lite`
- Feature or onboarding: `clean-saas-3d`, `soft-glass`, `premium-minimal`
- Small icons: `flat-product-icon`
- Premium SaaS: `premium-minimal`, `soft-glass`, `clean-saas-3d`
- Warm/friendly products: `friendly-clay`, `playful-mascot-lite`, `soft-glass`

## Built-ins

### premium-minimal

Restrained SaaS product assets with polished materials, quiet confidence, and generous whitespace.

### soft-glass

Translucent, product-ready glass forms with delicate reflections and a clean interface-friendly feel.

### friendly-clay

Warm clay-like 3D shapes with approachable product personality and simple readable silhouettes.

### clean-saas-3d

Crisp, modern 3D product illustrations for SaaS screens, feature cards, and onboarding surfaces.

### flat-product-icon

Simple flat vector-like product icons optimized for small UI sizes and immediate recognition.

### playful-mascot-lite

A light mascot-inspired direction that adds warmth while staying polished enough for product UI.

## Custom Styles

When a user describes a custom direction, save it as:

```text
profiles/style/<hyphen-case-name>.yaml
```

Keep custom styles reusable. Include keywords, materials, lighting, composition, defaults, forbidden patterns, and best-fit asset types.
